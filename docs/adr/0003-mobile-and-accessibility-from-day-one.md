# 3. Mobile, tablet and accessibility are v1 requirements

The app must be usable on phone, tablet and desktop, and accessible, from the first version. The owner wants to use it without a laptop and to share it publicly.

Consequence: the canvas editor cannot be the only way to perceive or edit a circuit. Interaction design must work with touch, mouse and keyboard alike, and the circuit needs a non-canvas representation that assistive technology can read.
